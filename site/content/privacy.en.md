## What we collect
- **Account:** your email address, used to sign in.
- **Purchases:** pack, amount, date and Tap's payment reference. Card details are handled by Tap Payments; we never receive full card numbers.
- **Usage:** which app and AI model you used, how many credits each request used, and the time.
- **What you send to our AI apps:** your prompts and the AI's replies, needed to give you the answer.

## Who we share it with
- **Tap Payments**, to process payments.
- **Supabase**, which hosts our login and database.
- **Render**, which hosts our servers.
- **AI providers**: your prompts are sent through **OpenRouter** or **Vercel AI Gateway** to the AI model you choose (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google). We set these services, where they allow it, not to log your prompts and not to use them for training.

We don't sell your personal data.
