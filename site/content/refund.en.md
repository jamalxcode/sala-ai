_Last updated: [DATE]_

- **Unused packs:** if you haven't used any credits from a pack, you can ask for a full refund within **14 days** of buying it.
- **Partly used packs:** these are not refundable, except where Kuwaiti consumer law requires it.
- **Expired credits:** these are not refundable.
- **How refunds are paid:** only to the original card or payment method, through Tap. We never pay refunds in cash or to a different account.
- **Failed or duplicate payments:** if you were charged but didn't receive credits, contact us and we'll fix it or refund you.
- **Chargebacks:** if you dispute a payment with your bank, the credits from that payment are removed. If they were already used, your account may be paused until the matter is resolved.

To ask for a refund, email **[support email]** from your account email address with the date and amount of the purchase. We reply within **[3] working days**.
